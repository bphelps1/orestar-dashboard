Cash on Hand vs ORESTAR

Every committee's calculated cash on hand, checked against the ending balance on its own ORESTAR account summary. Cash on hand is always calculated from transactions — ORESTAR is the check, never the input — so a gap means either our transaction data is incomplete or ORESTAR's own arithmetic is.

Dormant committees have no transactions in the year ORESTAR reports. ORESTAR carries their balance forward anyway, so a gap there usually means missing history rather than something wrong today.
