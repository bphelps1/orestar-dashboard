    Cash on Hand ?Calculated from: Beginning balance + cash contributions + other receipts − cash expenditures − other disbursements, summed across all recorded transactions.
    Beginning balance: For committees that pre-date ORESTAR (before 2006), the beginning balance is their "Beginning Balance (Previous Year)" from the earliest year on record. Committees created in 2006 or later start at $0 since all their transactions are captured.
    Meaning: How much liquid cash a committee currently holds, calculated entirely from transaction-level data. If a warning icon appears, it shows the difference between this calculation and the official ORESTAR ending cash balance.
